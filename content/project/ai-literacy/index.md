This project cluster covers my broader research on **how people learn about, with, and through AI**.

Representative directions include:

- reviewing how **prompt engineering** is being used in K-12 STEM education,
- studying how **generative AI and adaptive learning** can be combined,
- designing **personalized analogies** to make AI concepts more accessible to novices.

The common thread is building theory-informed ways to make AI systems understandable, educationally useful, and aligned with real learner needs.
