Besides building AI systems for learning, I also care about a more basic question: **how do people learn AI itself?**

This project direction focuses on AI literacy, prompt engineering, and ways of explaining AI concepts to learners who are new to the topic.

## Main ideas in this line of work

- studying how **prompt engineering** is actually used in K-12 STEM education,
- reviewing what kinds of prompting strategies work and where evidence is still weak,
- using **personalized analogies** to make AI concepts easier for adult novices to understand,
- connecting these questions back to broader learning science theories.

## Why these papers belong together

Even though one paper is a systematic review and another is an intervention study, both are about making AI more understandable and teachable.

I see this project cluster as part of the foundation work. If we want AI to be broadly useful in education, then learners and educators both need better ways to understand what these systems are and how to work with them.
