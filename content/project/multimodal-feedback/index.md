A lot of educational AI work focuses on text only. I think that is useful, but not enough.

This project direction explores what happens when AI feedback becomes **multimodal** and **interactive**. Instead of only generating text, these systems combine slides, grading workflows, animation, voice, and richer interfaces to support learning.

## What is inside this cluster

- **VTutor** explores animated pedagogical agents with speech, lip sync, and expressive behaviors.
- **SlideItRight** studies whether retrieving relevant slides together with AI feedback improves learning.
- **Avalon** designs a human-in-the-loop grading workflow with instructor calibration and student self-assessment.
- **MuFIN** frames the broader idea of automating multimodal feedback generation with generative AI.

## Why these belong together

All of these projects share the same intuition: feedback should be more than a paragraph of generated text.

Sometimes the right support is a relevant slide. Sometimes it is a speaking avatar. Sometimes it is a calibrated grading workflow that keeps humans in the loop. Together, these projects explore how AI feedback can become more actionable, expressive, and trustworthy.
